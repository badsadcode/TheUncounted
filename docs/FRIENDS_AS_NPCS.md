# Friends as NPCs

Friends from Toasty's community, now in the game (with placeholder sprites until Noor draws them): see HANDOFF.md,
"Friends from Toasty's streams". Eranu waits in the Lantern Inn (quest "Cothepoo, Come Home"), Coth is camped in the
Whistling Wood, Dunstan practises in the square. Eranu and Coth join after the quest; Dunstan any time.

| Name | Look and feel (from the user) |
|---|---|
| **Coth** (Cothfotmeoo) | Looks like a castaway lost a thousand years ago: scruffy, skinny, long dark hair, a long heavy black beard. A warrior who is usually missing his trousers (from the group's zombie-survival games, where he loses them often, winter included); he. |
| **Eranu** | From Scotland. A brave, smart blonde warrior; she. |
| **Dunstan** | Young warrior with a giant, heavy sword; he. |

## In-jokes
- Coth and Eranu joke that Coth is Eranu's waifu. She sometimes calls him **Cothepoo**.
  Ideas: Eranu introduces Coth as "my waifu" with total confidence while Coth protests (still without trousers); she
  calls him Cothepoo in front of strangers; if both are in the party, banter between them; a quest where Eranu asks you
  to find Coth's trousers and Coth would rather you didn't.
- Coth the castaway: maybe he really was lost for a thousand years, found on a shore or in a cave, out of date on
  everything ("Is the king still Aldric?" "Which one?"). Fits the game's theme: like the player, he slipped out of the
  records.
- The trousers: never wears them, whatever the weather. In winter or in the snow he has an excuse ready ("Builds
  character"); an item, the Trousers of Coth, that he refuses to take back.
