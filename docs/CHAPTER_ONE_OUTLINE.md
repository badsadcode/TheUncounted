# Chapter One: The City Formerly Known As (outline for approval)

## The hook

Chapter Zero ends with the Uncounted Ledger: during the Great Silence the Archivists struck **seven cities** off every
map and record to hide them from the Hunger (the Name-Eater). The player, struck off too, is the only kind of person the
Hunger can't see, and the only kind who can find a struck city.

- **Bureau ending**: a sealed summons arrives for Clerk Oakhaven. The High Chroniclers want an "uncounted person of
  good standing" to audit the first of the seven cities. Oakhaven has never been so excited about a form.
- **Ashbound ending**: Kaelen reads the first page of the Ledger aloud at dusk, and a milestone that wasn't there before
  appears on the Old Road.

Both lead to the same place: a side road off the Old Road that only appears **at dusk** (the new day and night cycle in
use), to a city whose name was eaten. Its signs say **[CITY NAME HERE]**. The people who live there call it **Here**.

## The place: Here

A whole city behind a bank of fog, real and lived in, but forgotten by the world for four hundred years.

- **By day it's faded**: half-transparent, people flicker, doors won't open. **At night it's solid.** The chapter is
  played mostly after dark and the clock matters (wait, T, until dusk).
- **The Gate**: a customs post for a city that doesn't exist, staffed by **Gatekeeper Ottoline Pell**, who has waited
  four hundred years for someone to bring the right paperwork.
- **The Market of the Unwritten**: residents who've lost their own names and go by descriptions: *The Baker,
  Probably*; *Person in a Hat*; *Small Loud Child*; *The Cartographer (Retired)*.
- **The Archive Tower**: where the city's name was kept, now hollowed out by the Hunger's **Mouths**.
- A built **blockout** (simple shapes, real collision, lights, monsters, quest objects) that you can later rebuild in
  your own style in Blender, like the village.

## The quest line (main campaign: Chapter One)

1. **The Road That Wasn't** — find the dusk milestone on the Old Road; follow the road into the fog.
2. **Visitor's Permit** — Ottoline won't let anyone in without Form 1 (Entry, Municipal, Non-Existent). Fill it in
   (dialogue with real choices: what is your name, is it legally yours, are you currently dead).
3. **Pieces of a Name** — the city's name survives in fragments in three people's memories, each a small quest:
   - *The Baker, Probably* remembers its **smell** (bake the city's bread from a recipe found in the ruins).
   - *Small Loud Child* remembers its **song** (find the verses chalked on walls around the city at night).
   - *The Cartographer (Retired)* remembers its **shape** (climb the bell tower and look at the streets by moonlight).
4. **The Archive Tower** — a dungeon: forms, filing puzzles, the Mouths, and the empty plinth where the name was kept.
5. **Say It, Write It, or Keep It** — with the three fragments you know the name. Three endings:
   - **Say it aloud**: the city returns to every map; the Hunger hears and comes: boss fight, **The Unsaying**.
   - **Write it in the Ledger**: the city stays hidden but whole; its people get their own names back, quietly.
   - **Give it to whoever has the Ledger** (Bureau or Ashbound, from Chapter Zero's ending) and let them decide.

Every ending feeds the chapter's epilogue, and the city's name appears (or doesn't) on the world map afterwards.
